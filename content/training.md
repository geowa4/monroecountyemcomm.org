Continuous training is essential for effective emergency communications. We recommend the following courses for all members to enhance their skills and preparedness.

## FEMA Independent Study Courses

These courses provide essential knowledge for working within the incident command structure during emergencies:

- **[IS-100.C: Introduction to the Incident Command System](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-100.c)** - Learn the fundamentals of ICS for effective emergency response coordination.

- **[IS-200.C: Basic Incident Command System for Initial Response](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-200.c)** - Build on ICS basics with skills for initial response operations.

- **[IS-700.B: An Introduction to the National Incident Management System](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-700.b)** - Understand NIMS and its role in national emergency management.

- **[IS-800.D: National Response Framework, An Introduction](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-800.d)** - Learn about the NRF and how communities respond to disasters.

## Amateur Radio Emergency Communications

Specialized training for amateur radio operators in emergency service:

- **[ARRL Emergency Communications Training](http://www.arrl.org/emergency-communications-training)** - ARRL's comprehensive emergency operations courses, including integrated FEMA and Skywarn training.

- **[AuxComm Training](https://www.auxcommusa.org/training)** - Auxiliary communications training program for emergency communicators.

## Getting Started

All courses listed above are available online at no cost and can be completed at your own pace. We encourage new members to complete the FEMA IS-100, IS-200, IS-700, and IS-800 courses as a foundation for emergency communications work.
