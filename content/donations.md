Your donations help Monroe County ARES/RACES maintain our capability to provide critical emergency communications services to our community. Even a small contribution can go a long way toward deferring our operational expenses.

## How Your Donations Help

Your generous contributions support essential operational costs including:

- **Liability Insurance** - Protecting our volunteers while they serve the community
- **Post Office Box Fees** - Maintaining our official mailing address
- **Web Page Expenses** - Keeping our online presence active and informative

## Suggested Donation

We suggest a donation of **$10**, though any amount is greatly appreciated and helps support our mission.
