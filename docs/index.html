Welcome to Monroe County ARES/RACES

Monroe County ARES (Amateur Radio Emergency Service) is a group of dedicated Amateur Radio operators committed to providing emergency communications support to our community. We are part of the larger Amateur Radio Emergency Service network that provides critical communication capabilities during emergencies and disasters when normal communication systems may fail or become overloaded.

Who We Are

We are licensed amateur radio operators who volunteer our time, equipment, and expertise to serve Monroe County and the surrounding areas in emergency situations. Our members come from all walks of life, united by our commitment to public service and our passion for amateur radio.

What We Do

  • Emergency Communications: Provide backup communication services during natural disasters, emergencies, and public events
  • Public Service: Support community events, marathons, and other activities requiring reliable communications
  • Training: Regular training sessions and exercises to maintain readiness and improve skills
  • Community Outreach: Education about amateur radio and emergency preparedness

Meetings

We meet monthly except during July, August, and December. All licensed amateur radio operators are welcome to join our meetings and learn more about emergency communications.

Get Involved

Whether you’re a new ham operator or an experienced amateur radio enthusiast, we welcome you to join our mission of serving the community through emergency communications. To become a member, please email secretary@monroecountyemcomm.org. Together, we help keep Monroe County connected when it matters most.
