Join Our Network

If you’re a licensed amateur radio operator interested in emergency communications and public service, we’d love to hear from you. Whether you’re newly licensed or have years of experience, there’s a place for you in Monroe County ARES/RACES. To become a member, please email secretary@monroecountyemcomm.org.

Emergency Communications

For actual emergency communications needs, please contact your local emergency management agency. Monroe County ARES/RACES operates under the direction of served agencies during emergency situations.
