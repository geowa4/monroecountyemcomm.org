Training

Continuous training is essential for effective emergency communications. We recommend the following courses for all members to enhance their skills and preparedness.

FEMA Independent Study Courses

These courses provide essential knowledge for working within the incident command structure during emergencies:

  • IS-100.C: Introduction to the Incident Command System - Learn the fundamentals of ICS for effective emergency response coordination.

  • IS-200.C: Basic Incident Command System for Initial Response - Build on ICS basics with skills for initial response operations.

  • IS-700.B: An Introduction to the National Incident Management System - Understand NIMS and its role in national emergency management.

  • IS-800.D: National Response Framework, An Introduction - Learn about the NRF and how communities respond to disasters.

Amateur Radio Emergency Communications

Specialized training for amateur radio operators in emergency service:

  • ARRL Emergency Communications Training - ARRL’s comprehensive emergency operations courses, including integrated FEMA and Skywarn training.

  • AuxComm Training - Auxiliary communications training program for emergency communicators.

Getting Started

All courses listed above are available online at no cost and can be completed at your own pace. We encourage new members to complete the FEMA IS-100, IS-200, IS-700, and IS-800 courses as a foundation for emergency communications work.

For questions about training requirements or to share your completed certifications with the organization, please contact secretary@monroecountyemcomm.org.
